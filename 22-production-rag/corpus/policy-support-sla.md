# Support SLAs

## First response targets

| Plan       | P1 (outage) | P2 (degraded) | P3 (question) |
|------------|-------------|---------------|---------------|
| Free       | Community forum only | — | — |
| Pro        | 4 hours     | 8 hours       | 24 hours      |
| Enterprise | 1 hour, 24/7 | 4 hours      | 12 hours      |

## Severity definitions

**P1** means production is down or data is at risk for a majority of your users.
**P2** means a significant feature is unavailable but a workaround exists.
**P3** is everything else, including questions and feature requests.

Customers may not self-assign P1. Support reclassifies severity on first response,
and the SLA clock runs from the reclassified severity.
