# Security Disclosure Policy

## Reporting

Send reports to security@example.com with reproduction steps. We acknowledge within
one business day and give an initial assessment within five.

## Our commitments

We will not pursue legal action against researchers acting in good faith who avoid
privacy violations, data destruction, and service degradation. Testing against other
customers' data is never in good faith.

## Disclosure timeline

We aim to ship a fix within 90 days and ask that you hold public disclosure until
then, or until a fix ships, whichever is sooner. Critical vulnerabilities affecting
customer data are targeted at 14 days.
