# Refund Policy

## Eligibility

| Plan type | Window | Basis        |
|-----------|--------|--------------|
| Monthly   | 14 days from the charge | Full refund |
| Annual    | 30 days from the charge | Pro-rata    |

Refunds outside these windows require director approval and are granted only for
billing errors on our side.

## Process

Support agents may not promise a refund. The correct phrasing is that the request
will be reviewed within two business days. Approved refunds return to the original
payment method and take 5–10 business days to settle.

## Cancellation

Cancelling stops future charges but does not itself trigger a refund. Access
continues until the end of the paid period. There are no partial-month credits.
